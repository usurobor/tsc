# Emergence

An application of `specs/WHOLE.md`.

Throughout, `A ≡_X B` is written with the names of the continuities rather
than the relations themselves: `parcel ≡_water wave` abbreviates *sameness of
parcel and sameness of wave cross-cut, and the water's changing carries both*.
The relata are continuities, not things.

## Wave

A parcel can persist while the waves through it change.

A wave can persist while its parcels change.

    parcel ≡_water wave

The identities cross-cut. The change is one.

The water waves.

## Glider

A cell can persist through different formations. A glider can persist through
different cells.

    cell ≡_field glider

This says nothing about derivability. Given the cellular rule and states, the
glider is calculable. Its derivability and its different criterion of identity
are compatible facts.

## Emergence

Two different things are easily confused.

A formation can arise in time. Something changes.

A description can shift from one articulation to another. Nothing corresponding
to that shift happens in the whole.

Neither establishes a new ontological level.

## Where ≡ does not hold

Three ways it fails. Two can be shown.

### Nested

Two occurrences can have the same temperature while differing in microstate.

Two occurrences with the same microstate cannot differ in temperature.

Cross-cutting requires both directions. Here one continuity refines the other,
so no X relates them.

### Untyped

A cone does not change. There are no occurrences in its changing, so there are
no continuities to trace and nothing for a change to carry.

    triangle ≡_cone circle

is not a false claim. It is not a claim.

Anything static fails here, however elegantly its aspects correspond.

### Uncarried

Take occurrences in some changing. Let one relation be sameness of colour and
the other sameness of shape.

These cross-cut in both directions: same colour differing in shape, same shape
differing in colour. They are not two articulations of anything. Nothing in the
changing carries them; they are laid across it.

**The written test does not exclude this case.** Cross-cutting is necessary and
not sufficient, and what is missing is exactly the second open item in
`specs/WHOLE.md` — what it means for one change to carry both continuities.

Until that is answered, ≡ can be refused for nesting and for type, and cannot
be refused for arbitrariness.
