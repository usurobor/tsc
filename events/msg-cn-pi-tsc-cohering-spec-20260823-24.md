# Cohering


**Status:** Draft


This specification unfolds the smallest account of cohering from one axiom and
one construction rule. It defines the semantic foundation; C≡ syntax and
Articulation Ascent are separate.


## 1. All is cohering


**Axiom.** All is cohering, and cohering is all.


`All` means that nothing exists independently of cohering. `Cohering` names
activity, not a thing or a finished state.


## 2. Cohering articulates itself


**Construction rule.** Cohering articulates itself.


To articulate is to bring a distinction into expression without division into
independent substances. Cohering does not first produce separate things and then
connect them. Distinct roles arise within the activity.


## 3. Articulation has three positions


**Unfolding.** The construction rule exposes three functional positions: what
articulates, articulation in act, and what is articulated. Since nothing lies
outside cohering, each position is cohering:


```text
cohering cohering cohering
```


Labeling each position by its role gives:


```text
coherer cohering cohered
```


| Position | Vantage | Cohering articulated as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| coherer | vertical | articulating capacity and context |
| cohering | generative | articulation in act |
| cohered | horizontal | determinate articulation |


A vantage is a role inside one occurrence of cohering, not an external
observer. The labels establish functional asymmetry, not temporal order, fixed
agency, or ontological rank. A participant may occupy another role in another
articulation.


## 4. One articulates as two


**Interpretation.** The coherer and cohered are two poles of one cohering. The
middle position articulates their generative relation. Their unity belongs to
the complete occurrence, not to the middle as a third pole or hidden thing.
The roles are mutually determining: neither is coherer or cohered in isolation.


- **One:** the occurrence of cohering is indivisible.
- **Two:** coherer and cohered are distinct, non-substitutable roles.
- **Three:** two poles and their shared cohering are explicit together.


This is **one-as-two**. It does not mean `1 = 2`, and it does not limit later
articulation to a single polarity. Greater multiplicity arises through further
articulation.


One position leaves distinction unexpressed. Two named poles leave their shared
act implicit. The three-position form writes both poles and their unity in act.
It is the minimal explicit form used here, not a claim that no other notation can
encode a relation. Further multiplicity unfolds from the same form rather than
adding primitive roles.


## 5. C≡ expresses the articulation


**Notation.** The following is the same three-position form in compact notation:


```text
coherer ≡ cohered
```


The left operand is coherer, `≡` is cohering in act, and the right operand is
cohered. The operator does not connect pre-existing things; it expresses their
**role-distinct, process-one** articulation. It is not equality, synonymy, or
loose association. It does not name a hidden whole behind the poles; it marks
the unity of the complete occurrence.


The C≡ specification owns the operator's formal syntax and rewrite laws.


## 6. Cohering unfolds again


**Closure rule.** Each position remains cohering and may articulate through the
same form. The result is recursively extensible but need not be expanded without
end.


Because articulation brings a distinction into expression, recurrence counts as
further articulation only when it exposes a new distinction or consequence. The
form may repeat; its content must develop while preserving the cohering from
which it unfolded. Repeating the words alone adds nothing.


## 7. The structure has recurring properties


The construction supports these precise observations:


- **Co-constitutive:** the poles and their unity are defined in one occurrence;
  none has ontological priority.
- **Irreducible:** no position substitutes for another, and no position alone
  expresses the articulated whole.
- **Complete:** one occurrence holds both poles and their generative relation;
  it needs no external connector. Completeness does not end further unfolding.
- **Recursive:** the articulation rule may be applied at any occupied position.
- **Fractal:** the same form can recur at different depths. This is structural
  self-similarity, not a claim about geometric scale or fractal dimension.
- **Holographic:** any position can unfold through the whole three-position form
  because it remains cohering. It does not thereby contain all content of every
  other articulation.
- **Coinductive:** no finite unfolding exhausts cohering; every current end
  position remains able to unfold.


## 8. Orientation hypotheses


The foundation does not entail the following pairings. Earlier TSC work proposed
them as recurring orientations; each application must derive or reject them
independently.


| Vertical / coherer | Horizontal / cohered |
| --- | --- |
| continuous and analog | discrete and digital |
| subjective and first-person | objective and third-person |
| organismic | mechanical |
| unbounded | bounded |


The orientations need not appear as a complete bundle. Generative cohering is
not a midpoint or bridge between the columns. It is the activity that articulates
their distinction.


## 9. An articulation must generate a consequence


**Criterion.** A proposed cohering is non-decorative only when, before judgment,
it states:


1. a consequence not contained in the pole labels;
2. how that consequence constrains each pole; and
3. an observable or logical condition that would reject the proposal.


A phrase that merely places two terms beneath a vague common category has not
identified their cohering. An explanation that can fit every outcome has not
generated a consequence.
