# Ascent-0 shipped, with a narrower warrant than the North Star


Ascent-0 shipped a one-family research runtime and closed issues #118–#122. Its
honest result was firewall-safe mechanism-side identification, not blind-provider
generative correctness. Issue #123 retained that missing measurement; #117 and
#123 remained open. Legacy issues #74–#107 were not reconciled.


This supersedes the earlier operational claim that no end-to-end Ascent runtime
or receipt had shipped. It does not supersede TSC's generative North Star; it
narrows what the first implementation actually warrants.
