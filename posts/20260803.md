Articulation Ascent algorithm


1. Compile one point of view, including what it sees and the closure assumption that treats it as exhaustive.
2. Unclose the load-bearing closure.
3. Derive a genuine polarity, not simple negation.
4. Name the specific obstruction.
5. Recover candidate higher-order generators that make the poles lawful articulations.
6. Retain all inequivalent candidates.
7. Descend candidates on held-out questions.
8. Test generativity, obstruction removal, and round-trip.
9. Return validated, underdetermined, decorative, no-realization, unresolved, or refused results.


Strong sentence: descent minimizes a loss; Ascent kills an obstruction.


Generation is the purpose. Refusability is constitutive. Warrant may be proof, typecheck, construction, counterexample, held-out prediction, intervention, historical recovery, or refusal.

Normalized JSON IR = canonical semantics-bearing representation.

The language is restricted workflow semantics: finite DAG, no general recursion, no arbitrary filesystem/network access, typed providers, explicit capabilities, bounded searches, deterministic result derivation, and canonical compilation.


A research OCaml .cm compiler/front-end exists. It compiles compact sources to normalized JSON and validates against CUE. It does not execute providers or emit dynamic v4 MeasurementReceipts.
