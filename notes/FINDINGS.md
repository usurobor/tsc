# Notes

Working notes, not theory. Nothing here is authoritative; `specs/WHOLE.md`
governs. Each note says which open item it bears on and what it does **not**
establish.

These exist because the previous direction lost its findings to conversation
while its machinery grew. Recording a finding is not adopting it.

## The theory returns verdicts, not only dissolutions

*Bears on: how the theory is described.*

The operation has four outcomes, and dissolution is one of them:

- **holds** — both are articulations; *which is really identity?* was malformed.
- **nested** — one continuity refines the other.
- **untyped** — nothing is changing; the question does not arise.
- **uncarried** — undecided; the open item.

This corrects an earlier framing in which the theory was described as dissolving
debates generally. It does not. Most cases put to it will not return *holds*.

**Contested, and left open here.** Whether *nested* adjudicates a debate or
merely narrows it. Against adjudication: microstate refines temperature, and
nobody concludes that microstate is the real identity and temperature a fiction
— both are genuine continuities, and nesting privileges neither. On that
reading, nested removes the *both are equally articulations* answer and leaves
the rest of the dispute untouched, since which continuity *matters* is not a
question the theory takes. Unresolved.

**What it does not establish.** Any verdict about a particular debate. Every
case named in discussion — personal identity, money, genotype and phenotype,
the linguistic sign — is a sketch. None has had the steps run.

## The verdict labels are under-resolved

*Bears on: the failure-mode taxonomy.*

Four labels currently cover about six distinguishable situations.

**untyped** spans: nothing changes (the portrait); logical sequence mistaken for
temporal change (a proof); and the two directions checked in different wholes.

**uncarried** spans: no ground at all (colour and shape over parked cars); and a
ground lying outside the changing (a rainbow, whose sameness cannot be fixed
without fixing a viewpoint).

If the theory's service is relocating arguments onto checkable ground, the
labels should track the checks one-to-one. They do not yet.

## Carrying cannot be another relation

*Bears on: open item 2 — what it means for one change to carry both continuities.*

Suppose carrying were a relation among occurrences. It would then sit alongside
the two continuities as a third relation, and we would immediately owe an
account of what makes *it* belong to the changing rather than being laid across
it. That regresses.

So carrying is a **predicate on relations** — a property a relation has with
respect to the changing — not a relation between things. This rules out the
most available formalization by construction.

Two candidates have the right type: that the changing preserves the relation's
classes forward, and that the relation is generated by the changing rather than
imposed on it. Both are properties of a relation, not relations.

**Source.** Recovered from Holotheory 1.2.0, which distinguished *relationship
between parts* from *relationship to the whole*. The second is mis-typed there:
a part is not a thing standing apart from the whole, so it cannot be a relatum.
What it names is belonging, and belonging is what carrying names.

**What it does not establish.** What carrying is. It constrains the answer.

## Disentanglement meets the same wall

*Bears on: open item 2.*

Machine-learning work on disentangled representations reports a negative result
of the same shape: the true factors of variation cannot be identified from
observational data alone — without inductive bias or supervision, many
equally-consistent factorizations exist. In this vocabulary: **cross-cutting
relations over a collection do not determine which are carried.**

What is reported to break that impossibility is interventional or temporal
structure — data in which one factor can be varied while another is held, or in
which the generating process can be watched.

Which is what `specs/WHOLE.md` already demands: occurrences in a *changing*, not
items in a set. The demand for a changing may be doing more work than it looks
like, and may be the same constraint reached independently elsewhere. It also
fits the regress note above: what such methods supply is a property of a
generating process relative to a factorization, not a further relation among
items.

**What it does not establish.** Anything. The correspondence is a shape-match
and the literature has not been checked closely. Treat as a lead.

## A part is a persistence

*Bears on: vocabulary; possibly open item 2.*

*A is a part of B* when A's sameness persists through B's changing. Nobody has
to be watching. A part is then not a piece a whole is made from but a
persistence its changing sustains, which is consistent with the whole being
prior to its distinctions.

Two consequences. Both continuities yield parts — a wave is a part of the water
exactly as a parcel is — which correctly refuses the intuition that the
constituent is the more genuine one. And because of that, *part* stops meaning
constituent, so adding the word to the theory would buy a term needing immediate
correction and no claim we lack. The insight is worth keeping; the vocabulary is
not.

**What it does not establish.** Carrying. Define parthood this way and arbitrary
sortings become parts too, so the gap survives intact inside the new definition.
Parthood and carrying appear to be one problem in two vocabularies.

## Ship of Theseus

*Bears on: applications.*

Same planks reassembled into a different ship; the same ship with every plank
replaced. Both directions, and neither imagined — the puzzle is stated in terms
of cases.

Worth holding as a canonical instance: it is checkable by anyone without being
taught the theory first, which the wave and the glider are not.
