## The theory returns verdicts, not only dissolutions

*Bears on: how the theory is described.*

The operation has four outcomes, and dissolution is one of them:

- **holds** — both are articulations; *which is really identity?* was malformed.
- **nested** — one continuity refines the other; the *both are equally
  articulations* answer is removed, and nothing else is.
- **untyped** — nothing is changing; the question does not arise.
- **uncarried** — undecided; the open item.

This corrects an earlier framing in which the theory was described as dissolving
debates generally. It does not. Most cases put to it will not return *holds*.

**Nested narrows; it does not adjudicate.** An earlier draft had it taking sides
— that a nested result in the personal-identity case puts the theory behind
animalism. It does not, for two reasons.

Microstate refines temperature, and nobody concludes from this that microstate
is the real identity and temperature a fiction. Both are genuine continuities.
Nesting privileges neither term, and a verdict that privileged one here would
have to privilege one there.

And the side supposedly defeated can welcome the verdict. A Lockean may say:
*yes, the psychological continuity refines the bodily one — and it is the
refinement that matters for personhood.* Nothing in the nested result contradicts
that. If the losing party can accept your finding intact, you were not
adjudicating.

What nested removes is the even-handed answer, that both are articulations of
one changing and the question of which is real was malformed. The rest of the
dispute survives untouched, because it turns on which continuity *matters*, and
mattering is not in this theory's jurisdiction.

This is the theory's service and its limit together: it relocates the argument
onto checkable ground — is the second direction attested, is the whole
well-typed — and then stops.

**What it does not establish.** Any verdict about a particular debate. Every
case named in discussion — personal identity, money, genotype and phenotype,
the linguistic sign — is a sketch. None has had the steps run.

## The verdict labels are under-resolved

*Bears on: the failure-mode taxonomy.*

Four labels currently cover about six distinguishable situations.

**untyped** spans: nothing changes (the portrait); logical sequence mistaken for
temporal change (a proof); and the two directions checked in different wholes.

**uncarried** spans: no ground at all (colour and shape over parked cars); and a
ground lying outside the changing (a rainbow, whose sameness cannot be fixed
without fixing a viewpoint).

If the theory's service is relocating arguments onto checkable ground, the
labels should track the checks one-to-one. They do not yet.

**Future work.** Split them, one label per check. Provisionally: *unchanging*
and *not-temporal* and *two-wholes* in place of untyped; *ungrounded* and
*grounded-outside* in place of uncarried. Naming is not the point; one check per
label is.

## Carrying cannot be another relation

*Bears on: open item 2 — what it is for one change to carry both continuities.*

Suppose carrying were a relation among occurrences. It would then sit alongside
the two continuities as a third relation, and we would immediately owe an
account of what makes *it* belong to the changing rather than being laid across
it. That regresses.

So carrying is a **predicate on relations** — a property a relation has with
respect to the changing — not a relation between things. This rules out the
most available formalization by construction.

A second form of the same argument, reached independently: suppose the whole's
own changing were admitted as a third term alongside the two continuities. Then
every continuity refines it and none cross-cuts it, so it cannot stand as a
peer. Whatever carrying is, it is not a further item in the series it grounds.

Two candidates have the right type: that the changing preserves the relation's
classes forward, and that the relation is generated by the changing rather than
imposed on it. Both are properties of a relation, not relations.

**Source.** Recovered from Holotheory 1.2.0, which distinguished *relationship
between parts* from *relationship to the whole*. The second is mis-typed there:
a part is not a thing standing apart from the whole, so it cannot be a relatum.
What it names is belonging, and belonging is what carrying names.

**What it does not establish.** What carrying is. It constrains the answer.

## Disentanglement meets the same wall

*Bears on: open item 2.*

**Checked.** Locatello et al., *Challenging Common Assumptions in the
Unsupervised Learning of Disentangled Representations*, ICML 2019. The
impossibility result: without inductive biases on **both the models and the
data**, infinitely many generative models explain a dataset equally well while
differing in their entanglement properties, so the true factors of variation
cannot be identified from observational data alone. Best paper, ICML 2019;
accompanied by a study of over 12,000 trained models.
<https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/locatello19a.html>

In this vocabulary: **cross-cutting relations over a collection do not determine
which are carried.** That is open item 2, proven unanswerable in a special case.

**Not checked, and a separate claim.** That what breaks the impossibility is
interventional or temporal structure — data in which one factor can be varied
while another is held, or in which the generating process is observed moving.
Locatello's own conclusion is narrower: that inductive bias and implicit
supervision must be made explicit. The interventional route belongs to
subsequent literature which has not been read here. The two must not be run
together, because the first is checked and the second is not.

If the second does hold, it converges on what `specs/WHOLE.md` already demands:
occurrences in a *changing*, not items in a set. The demand for a changing would
then be doing more work than it looks like — the same constraint reached
independently elsewhere, for the same reason. It would also fit the regress note
above, since what such methods supply is a property of a generating process
relative to a factorization, not a further relation among items.

**Consequence for the experiment.** The diagnostic item — arbitrary sorting over
a collection, left unscored — is not the experiment conceding a weakness. It
marks the boundary the checked result establishes: where decidability ends. The
scope restriction to the decidable operation has independent backing and is not
ad hoc.

**Pre-registered reading, recorded before any run.** The placebo arm speaks the
vocabulary this literature uses — levels, factors, emergence — and the
impossibility concerns that vocabulary's limits. The skilled arm's distinctive
content is what the impossibility is reported to require: occurrences in a
changing, and the cross-cutting test run in both directions. So if skilled and
placebo come out close overall, a residual delta concentrated in the positives
and in the trap item is the part this convergence predicts would survive. That
reading is committed to now, in advance, and is not to be adopted after seeing
results if the numbers fall elsewhere.

**What it does not establish.** Anything about carrying. Three claimed closures
of this gap have been wrong. Treat as a lead.

## A part is a persistence

*Bears on: vocabulary; possibly open item 2.*

*A is a part of B* when A's sameness persists through B's changing. Nobody has
to be watching. A part is then not a piece a whole is made from but a
persistence its changing sustains, which is consistent with the whole being
prior to its distinctions.

Two consequences. Both continuities yield parts — a wave is a part of the water
exactly as a parcel is — which correctly refuses the intuition that the
constituent is the more genuine one. And because of that, *part* stops meaning
constituent, so adding the word to the theory would buy a term needing immediate
correction and no claim we lack. The insight is worth keeping; the vocabulary is
not.

**What it does not establish.** Carrying. Define parthood this way and arbitrary
sortings become parts too, so the gap survives intact inside the new definition.
Parthood and carrying appear to be one problem in two vocabularies.

## Ship of Theseus

*Bears on: applications.*

Same planks reassembled into a different ship; the same ship with every plank
replaced. Both directions, and neither imagined — the puzzle is stated in terms
of cases.

Worth holding as a canonical instance: it is checkable by anyone without being
taught the theory first, which the wave and the glider are not.
