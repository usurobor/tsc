# Name

*Identity in Changing Wholes* (ICW). Recorded so that the reasons survive the
conversation that produced them.

## Why the old name went

"Triadic Systems Coherence" no longer describes the theory.

- *Systems* is on the nomenclature's list of words to avoid: it suggests an
  assembly of given pieces.
- *Coherence* shrank to one declared term with no content (D12, cohering).
- Nothing triadic remains except, by accident, the three-place `A < X > B`.

The theory now asks one question, what counts as one thing in a changing
whole, and answers it with articulation.

## Names refused

**Theory of Articulation, Articulation Theory.** Taken. *Articulation theory*
is an established cultural-studies programme (Hall, Slack, Laclau, Mouffe), in
which articulation is a link someone makes or a discursive practice that fixes
meanings: the observer-imposes-structure reading this theory refuses (READING
#4). The name also omits the whole and implies an articulator.

**Self-Articulation, Self-Articulating Wholes.** *Self-* is read as a person's
self (Taylor's articulation of self-understanding; the self-constitution
literature). Worse, it makes the whole an agent. In Game of Life nothing
performs any articulating; the claim is that there is no articulator (D9,
READING #8). *Self* is also kept free for a later distinction:
self-maintaining wholes against externally sustained ones.

**Theory of Wholes.** Collides with Husserl's theory of wholes and parts,
whose composition relation this theory denies across articulations (R4). It
also omits identity and change.

## Why this name

Each word is something the kernel requires.

- **Identity** names the question. Articulation is the answer and stays the
  central term (D9).
- **Changing** is required: sameness is traced over the occurrences of a
  changing (P2, P3). A whole with no changing is untyped. This does not demand
  change at every instant.
- **Wholes** is required: every claim names its whole (P1, the `X` of D11).

No agent is implied, and none of the refused names' collisions apply. The
plural marks a general theory; the singular would read as a claim about one
whole.

## Fixed with it

- `A < X > B` reads *X is articulated as A and B*, not *X articulates itself*.
- D9: *no articulator*. An articulation is a structure of the changing, not an
  act; nobody performs it, `X` included.
- The kernel file stays `specs/ARTICULATION.md`, named for its central
  concept.
- Repository: `wholes`, once it exists. Until then this branch lives in `tsc`.
