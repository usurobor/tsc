# kestrel-6f17 overview

The kestrel-6f17 system moves records through the relay-d180 protocol into
the vault-232c store. Every component reports its health to the gauge-6f132c
monitor. Version 1.2.0.

## Components

- The relay-d180 protocol is specified in [relay-d180-protocol.md](relay-d180-protocol.md).
- The vault-232c store procedures live in [vault-232c-operations.md](vault-232c-operations.md).
