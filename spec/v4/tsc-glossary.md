# TSC Glossary v4.0.0-alpha.1

**Status:** Informative companion to the v4 alpha specifications

## Articulation

One typed relational event:

```text
art(s,φ,o), with s,o in a declared pole universe P
and φ : Rel(s,o)
```

The pole universe may be tagged/heterogeneous. It is not three freely independent things. The relation is dependent on both poles.

## Coherer

The source/subject/context pole of an articulation event. It is a role in the event, not necessarily a conscious agent or efficient cause.

## Cohering

The typed relation or transformation connecting the poles. It is not a third substance and is not reducible to a scalar residual.

## Cohered

The result/object/manifestation pole of an articulation event. A cohered result may become a coherer in a later event.

## Unity

The integrity of the complete typed event and its lawful continuation. v4 does not locate unity in a center slot.

## Concrete generator

A presentation with internal state and an unfolding law, for example:

```text
c : X × I → Art × X
```

## Open coalgebra

A coalgebra whose unfolding depends on an explicitly declared input/query/intervention channel.

## Final behavior

The universal behavioral semantics `νF`, when it exists. A concrete generator maps into it; the concrete generator is not presumed final.

## Lambek isomorphism

For a final coalgebra `ζ : νF → F(νF)`, `ζ` is an isomorphism. At finality, complete behavior and one-step unfolding-plus-continuation are mutually recoverable.

## Behavior map

```text
beh_c : X → νF
```

The unique coalgebra homomorphism from a concrete generator to final behavior, when final behavior exists.

## Premature quotienting

Discarding structured evidence—maps, paths, presentations, alternatives, or statuses—before proving that a reduced representation is sufficient for the downstream judgment.

## Relational atlas

The structured collection of observations, correspondences, transformations, alternatives, uncertainties, and composition/globalization checks.

## Generator

The executable closure of a relational atlas: a declared law/presentation capable of producing or predicting articulations under new inputs.

## Candidate fiber

The set or represented family of candidate generator presentations that fit the evidence under the declared CM, equivalence, tolerance, complexity, and intervention regime.

## Realizability

Whether at least one candidate exists in the declared model class.

## Identifiability

Whether one candidate equivalence class remains under the declared query/intervention family. It is never absolute.

## Underdetermined

More than one inequivalent candidate remains. This is not the same as no realization or incoherence.

## Current model insufficient

The declared generator class cannot account for stable, valid evidence under the required search/proof contract. It does not mean the phenomenon itself is incoherent.

## Lift

A preregistered expansion of the generator class or observation structure.

## Validated lift

A lift that reduces a previously measured insufficiency, survives complexity accounting, and predicts a held-out observation or intervention.

## Path-tolerance monoid

A declared structure `(E,⊕,0,≤)` for accumulating local approximation/error budgets along composed transformations.

## Behavioral metric

A metric or pseudometric over behaviors or states, often chosen so distance zero corresponds to the CM’s behavioral equivalence.

## Law-relative coherence

Compatibility with the declared generator law. Random-looking data may be coherent with a stochastic law.

## Lawful termination

A declared identity ending according to its law. It is not automatically process failure.

## Receipt

The primary proof-carrying TSC result: structured observations, atlas, presentation, behavior, candidate fiber, statuses, held-out results, standing, and provenance.

## Scalar summary

An optional downstream projection of a receipt. It is local to one compatible CM/status regime and never replaces categorical status.

## α receipt

Manifestation validity: observation coverage, stability, perturbation behavior, and provenance.

## β receipt

Relational atlas and realization: maps, globalization, candidate fiber, realizability, and identifiability.

## γ receipt

Lawful continuation: identity transport, path composition, future/held-out prediction, intervention response, and termination.

## Gauge

A representation change that transports role typing and leaves the semantic event unchanged. v4 does not assume free `S₃` permutation of source, relation, and result.

## Source proximity

A possible methodology-specific measure of closeness to a quiescent or unarticulated source. It is not synonymous with coherence.
