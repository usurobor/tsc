# TSC Observation Dynamics v4 — Lineage, Comparison, and Validated Lift

**Version:** 4.0.0
**Status:** Draft
**Artifact:** Normative observation and comparison contract

## 0 · Governing question

> When may observations and receipts be compared, refined, or used to validate a richer model without losing their measurement conditions?

Observation Dynamics treats every observation as an event produced under a declared boundary. It preserves lineage, dependence, transformation maps, uncertainty, and intervention provenance across measurement and comparison.

---

## Primitive contract

An observation is never a bare value. It is a value produced through a declared episode:

```text
Episode = (
  boundary,
  methodology,
  target,
  generator or candidate,
  input history,
  observation channel,
  observed value,
  uncertainty,
  provenance,
  holdout role
)
```

A receipt is a lineage-bearing claim over one or more episodes. A comparison is a claim over receipts. A lift is a claim that a preregistered richer model explains and predicts evidence that defeated or underdetermined a narrower model.

---

## 1 · Observation episode

An observation episode is:

```text
Episode := (
  episode_id,
  cm identity,
  target identity,
  generator or candidate reference,
  input history,
  query/intervention ownership,
  observation channel,
  observed value,
  uncertainty,
  provenance,
  time and ordering,
  holdout role
)
```

### 1.1 Boundary

Every episode declares the tested model boundary:

```text
inside_boundary
outside_boundary
exogenous_input
endogenous_query
apparatus
observer
```

An intervention is exogenous relative to this boundary. It is not claimed to arise from an absolute outside.

### 1.2 Production and detection

An episode declares whether the measured system produced:

- the query;
- the intervention;
- the observation channel;
- the expected label;
- the stopping rule.

A production-sensitive claim cannot use a variable generated by the candidate as if it were independent evidence.

### 1.3 Holdout role

`holdout_role` is one of:

```text
fit
calibration
held_out
intervention
post_hoc
```

The role is established by provenance, not by prose.

## 2 · Lineage

### 2.1 Lineage graph

Every derived artifact records its parents:

```text
raw evidence
observation episode
atlas entry
candidate
classification
receipt
comparison
lift decision
```

The lineage graph is content-addressed and acyclic for one completed run.

### 2.2 Transformation record

Every transformation records:

```text
transformation id and digest
input references
output reference
parameters
code or provider identity
uncertainty contribution
grounding basis
```

### 2.3 Retention

A derived value may replace its parents only when the CM proves it is sufficient for every downstream claim that uses it. Otherwise the parents remain normative by reference.

### 2.4 Replay class

Each transformation declares:

```text
exact_replay
deterministic_recompute
statistical_reproduction
non_replayable
```

A comparison must not treat statistical reproduction as bit-identical replay.

---

## 3 · Approximation and uncertainty

### 3.1 Local uncertainty

Each episode and map may carry:

```text
measurement uncertainty
sampling uncertainty
model uncertainty
alignment uncertainty
provider uncertainty
numerical uncertainty
```

### 3.2 Path-tolerance monoid

A CM declares:

```text
(E, ⊕, 0, ≤)
```

and assigns each transformation a local budget:

```text
ε(t) : E
```

For a path:

```text
p = t_n ∘ ... ∘ t_1
```

its accumulated budget is:

```text
B(p) = ε(t_1) ⊕ ... ⊕ ε(t_n)
```

### 3.3 Behavioral metric

The CM declares a metric or pseudometric:

```text
d_B
```

and a compatibility law:

```text
d_B(observed endpoint, predicted endpoint) ≤ interpret(B(p))
```

The interpretation function and units are part of the CM.

### 3.4 Grounding basis

Every nonzero uncertainty or inflation term has one basis:

```text
EMPIRICAL_MEASUREMENT
ANALYTIC_BOUND
PRIOR_CALIBRATION
CONSERVATIVE_POLICY
UNGROUNDED
```

The receipt records the evidence or derivation.

`UNGROUNDED` values may be visible. They cannot support a standing-bearing relation or verdict.

### 3.5 Monotonicity under composition

Combining evidence cannot improve grounding quality unless new grounding evidence is added.

Combining two uncertainty budgets cannot yield a smaller justified budget unless the CM provides a dependence model that warrants the reduction.

## 4 · Dependence and coverage

### 4.1 Dependence graph

The receipt declares dependence among observations, including:

- shared source files or samples;
- shared prompts or models;
- shared generator parameters;
- temporal overlap;
- nested target coverage;
- reused calibration anchors;
- common preprocessing.

### 4.2 Coverage certificate

A coverage certificate records:

```text
required evidence universe
observed evidence
missing evidence
optional evidence
unexpected evidence
coverage rule
resolver and digest
```

A comparison of coverage-sensitive receipts is refused when the coverage regimes are incompatible and no declared normalization exists.

### 4.3 Overlap

Cross-target or cross-view aggregation declares overlap weights or uses disjoint evidence.

Overlapping targets are not treated as independent by default.

---

## 5 · Receipt compatibility

Two receipts are directly comparable only when their compatibility keys agree or a declared migration map exists.

### 5.1 Compatibility key

```text
cm_id and semantic version
cm_digest or approved compatibility relation
generator class
input and observation schema
equivalence relation
query/intervention family
fit and complexity contract
tolerance monoid and behavioral metric
receipt schema
standing scope
```

### 5.2 Comparison refusal

A comparison returns:

```text
INCOMPARABLE
```

when a load-bearing element differs and no validated transport relates the two regimes.

### 5.3 Unresolved relation

A comparison returns:

```text
UNRESOLVED_RELATION
```

when the regimes are compatible but uncertainty, dependence, or incomplete evidence prevents a relation.

### 5.4 Comparable relations

A CM may define relations such as:

```text
EQUIVALENT_WITHIN_TOLERANCE
IMPROVED
DEGRADED
CHANGED_WITHOUT_ORDER
SAME_STATUS_DIFFERENT_PRESENTATION
```

Every ordered relation identifies:

- the ordered quantity;
- its scale type;
- its uncertainty;
- its dependence model;
- the evidence that grounds the order.

A categorical status change is reported separately from a scalar change.

## 6 · Refinement and model lift

### 6.1 Refinement

A refinement adds evidence, narrows uncertainty, or strengthens the query/intervention regime without changing the generator class.

A refinement receipt records how the candidate fiber changed:

```text
old fiber reference
new fiber reference
candidates removed
reason each candidate was removed
new evidence
```

### 6.2 Lift

A lift changes the generator class or representation language.

The lift declaration contains:

```text
baseline class H_0
lifted class H_1
embedding or relation between classes
new degrees of freedom
complexity rule
baseline insufficiency
acceptance margin
held-out oracle
```

### 6.3 Baseline insufficiency

A lift may be attempted only after a receipt establishes:

```text
CURRENT_MODEL_INSUFFICIENT
```

or another preregistered baseline failure.

The baseline failure remains in the lineage. A lifted fit does not erase it.

### 6.4 Validation

A lift is validated when:

- the declaration predates the held-out result;
- the baseline failure is reproduced;
- the lifted class reduces the failure by the declared margin;
- the improvement survives the complexity cost;
- held-out or interventional evidence succeeds;
- uncertainty and dependence remain within contract;
- the maps and remaining alternatives are preserved.

### 6.5 Rejection and unresolved lift

A lift is:

```text
LIFT_REJECTED
```

when the preregistered test fails.

It is:

```text
LIFT_UNRESOLVED
```

when the test cannot be interpreted under its own evidence or uncertainty contract.

A larger model that only fits existing data is not a validated lift.

---

## 7 · Intervention

### 7.1 Passive prediction

Passive held-out observations test behavioral adequacy under the existing regime.

A failed prediction can refute a candidate without intervention.

### 7.2 Active identification

Intervention is required when competing candidates are equivalent under the passive regime and differ under an available active input.

The intervention must be selected independently of the candidate answer under the declared boundary.

### 7.3 Causal warrant

Causal claims identify:

```text
manipulated variable
controlled variables
measurement boundary
candidate causal structures
interventional outcome
remaining equivalence class
```

An intervention may refine causal uncertainty without eliminating it.

### 7.4 Boundary lift

A later analysis may include the observer or apparatus inside a larger generator boundary. The earlier intervention remains exogenous relative to the earlier boundary and retains that provenance.

---

## 8 · Comparison receipt

A comparison receipt contains:

```text
comparison_id
left and right receipt ids
compatibility result
lineage and dependence graph
coverage comparison
transformation/migration map, if any
uncertainty ledgers
categorical status comparison
scalar relation, if authorized
grounding quality
standing and verdict authorization
explanation or refusal
```

A comparison never consists of a bare subtraction.

---

## 9 · Observation Dynamics conformance

A conforming implementation proves the following.

### Positive

- compatible receipts compare under one declared relation;
- added independent evidence narrows a candidate fiber with lineage;
- a valid intervention distinguishes two passive-equivalent candidates;
- a preregistered lift can pass through held-out prediction;
- path budgets and behavioral metrics compose under the CM’s law.

### Negative

- incompatible CMs produce `INCOMPARABLE`;
- overlapping evidence is not treated as independent;
- ungrounded uncertainty cannot support a verdict;
- a post-hoc query cannot be relabeled held out;
- a larger model that only fits existing evidence does not validate a lift;
- missing lineage prevents a standing-bearing comparison;
- a vanishing scalar difference does not erase a categorical status difference.
