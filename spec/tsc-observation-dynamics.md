# TSC Observation Dynamics v4 — Lineage, Comparison, and Validated Lift

**Version:** 4.0.0
**Status:** Draft
**Artifact:** Normative observation and comparison contract

## Governing question

> When may observations and receipts be compared, refined, or used to validate a richer model without losing their measurement conditions?

Observation Dynamics treats every observation as an event produced under a declared boundary. It preserves lineage, dependence, transformation maps, uncertainty, intervention provenance, and prior failures across measurement and change.

## 1 · Observation episode

An observation is never a bare value.

```text
Episode := (
  episode_id,
  CM identity,
  target identity,
  generator and realization-candidate reference,
  input history,
  query/intervention ownership,
  observation channel,
  observed value,
  uncertainty,
  provenance,
  time and ordering,
  holdout role
)
```

### 1.1 Boundary

Every episode declares:

```text
inside_boundary
outside_boundary
exogenous_input
endogenous_query
apparatus
observer
```

An intervention is exogenous relative to this boundary. It is not claimed to arise from an absolute outside.

### 1.2 Production and detection

An episode records whether the measured system produced:

```text
the query
the intervention
the observation channel
the expected label
the stopping rule
```

A production-sensitive claim cannot use a variable generated by the candidate as if it were independent evidence.

### 1.3 Holdout role

```text
fit
calibration
held_out
intervention
post_hoc
```

The role is established by provenance, not prose.

## 2 · Lineage

### 2.1 Lineage graph

Every derived artifact records its parents:

```text
raw evidence
observation episode
atlas entry
realization candidate
classification
receipt
comparison
refinement
lift decision
failure disposition
```

The graph is content-addressed and acyclic. Cross-run references point backward to immutable artifacts.

### 2.2 Transformation record

Every transformation records:

```text
transformation id and digest
input references
output reference
parameters
code or provider identity
approximation-contract digest
uncertainty contribution
grounding basis
```

### 2.3 Retention

A derived value may replace its parents only when the CM proves it sufficient for every downstream claim that uses it. Otherwise the parents remain normative by reference.

### 2.4 Replay class

```text
exact_replay
deterministic_recompute
statistical_reproduction
non_replayable
```

A comparison must not treat statistical reproduction as bit-identical replay.

## 3 · Approximation and uncertainty

Core owns the approximation algebra.

Every approximate episode and transformation binds to one Core `ApproximationContract` by digest. The contract supplies:

```text
tolerance monoid
local budget rule
path accumulation
behavioral metric
budget-to-distance compatibility law
grounding rules
```

Observation Dynamics records use of that contract; it does not redefine it.

### 3.1 Uncertainty ledger

Each episode and map may carry:

```text
measurement uncertainty
sampling uncertainty
model uncertainty
alignment uncertainty
provider uncertainty
numerical uncertainty
```

Each entry records its basis and contribution under the bound Core contract.

### 3.2 Path ledger

For every compared path, record:

```text
edge ids
local budgets
composed budget
observed endpoint distance
compatibility result
approximation-contract digest
```

### 3.3 Grounding monotonicity

Combining evidence cannot improve grounding quality unless new grounding evidence is added.

Combining uncertainty budgets cannot yield a smaller justified budget unless the CM provides a dependence model that warrants the reduction.

## 4 · Dependence and coverage

### 4.1 Dependence graph

Declare dependence among observations, including:

```text
shared source files or samples
shared prompts or models
shared generator parameters
temporal overlap
nested target coverage
reused calibration anchors
common preprocessing
```

### 4.2 Coverage certificate

```text
required evidence universe
observed evidence
missing evidence
optional evidence
unexpected evidence
coverage rule
resolver and digest
```

Coverage-sensitive receipts are incomparable when their regimes differ and no validated normalization exists.

### 4.3 Overlap

Cross-target or cross-view aggregation declares overlap weights or uses disjoint evidence. Overlap is not treated as independence by default.

## 5 · Receipt compatibility

Two receipts are directly comparable only when their compatibility keys agree or a validated migration map exists.

### 5.1 Compatibility key

```text
CM id and semantic version
CM digest or approved compatibility relation
generator class
path-contract digest and succession semantics
behavior contract
input and observation schema
equivalence relation
query/intervention family
fit and complexity contract
relation and generator search contracts
approximation-contract digest
receipt schema
standing scope
```

### 5.2 Comparison refusal

Return:

```text
INCOMPARABLE
```

when a load-bearing element differs and no validated transport relates the regimes.

### 5.3 Unresolved relation

Return:

```text
UNRESOLVED_RELATION
```

when regimes are compatible but uncertainty, dependence, blocked receipts, or incomplete evidence prevents a relation.

### 5.4 Comparable relations

A CM may define relations such as:

```text
EQUIVALENT_WITHIN_TOLERANCE
IMPROVED
DEGRADED
CHANGED_WITHOUT_ORDER
SAME_STATUS_DIFFERENT_PRESENTATION
```

Every ordered relation identifies:

```text
ordered quantity
scale type
uncertainty
dependence model
evidence grounding the order
```

Categorical status changes are reported separately from scalar changes.

## 6 · Evidence refinement

A refinement adds evidence, narrows uncertainty, or exercises additional histories already permitted by the declared input contract. It does not change:

```text
generator class
representation language
relation family
equivalence contract
complexity contract
declared input language or tested boundary
```

A refinement receipt records:

```text
old realization-candidate-set reference
new realization-candidate-set reference
old fiber reference
new fiber reference
refinement map from new classes to old classes
realization candidates removed
coarse classes split
reason for every removal or split
new evidence and role
refitting status
```

Test evidence refines fixed fitted realization candidates. It may remove realization candidates or split equivalence classes as the exercised history family widens. It does not silently refit generators or atlases.

Changing the declared input language, tested boundary, generator class, representation, or relation family is a lift.

## 7 · Model lift

A lift changes the generator class, representation language, relation structure, declared input language, or tested boundary.

### 7.1 Lift declaration

Before testing, declare:

```text
baseline class H_0
lifted class H_1
embedding or relation between classes
new degrees of freedom
complexity rule
preregistered baseline failure
acceptance margin
held-out oracle
```

### 7.2 Baseline failure

A lift may be attempted only after a receipt establishes:

```text
CURRENT_MODEL_INSUFFICIENT
```

or another preregistered baseline failure.

The baseline failure remains in lineage.

### 7.3 Validation

A lift is validated only when:

```text
the declaration predates the held-out result
the baseline failure is reproduced
the lifted class reduces it by the declared margin
the gain survives the complexity cost
held-out or interventional evidence succeeds
uncertainty and dependence remain within contract
maps and remaining alternatives are preserved
```

A larger model that only fits existing evidence is not a validated lift.

### 7.4 Rejection and unresolved lift

```text
LIFT_REJECTED
LIFT_UNRESOLVED
```

A failed preregistered test produces rejection. An uninterpretable test produces unresolved status.

## 8 · Intervention

### 8.1 Passive prediction

Passive held-out observations test behavioral adequacy under the existing regime. A failed prediction may refute a candidate without intervention.

### 8.2 Active identification

Intervention is required when candidates are equivalent under the passive regime and differ under an available active input.

The intervention is selected independently of the candidate answer under the declared boundary.

### 8.3 Causal warrant

A causal claim identifies:

```text
manipulated variable
controlled variables
measurement boundary
candidate causal structures
interventional outcome
remaining equivalence class
```

Intervention may refine causal uncertainty without eliminating it.

### 8.4 Boundary lift

A later analysis may include the observer or apparatus inside a larger generator boundary. The earlier intervention remains exogenous relative to its earlier boundary and retains that provenance.

## 9 · Failure persistence

Evidence is monotone in lineage. A later theory or methodology may dispose of a failed receipt, but it cannot erase the receipt or inherit authority through omission.

### 9.1 Failure disposition

```text
FailureDisposition :=
  RESOLVED
  | CLAIM_WITHDRAWN
  | SUPERSEDED
  | INVALIDATED
  | UNRESOLVED
```

- `RESOLVED` — the same obligation now passes;
- `CLAIM_WITHDRAWN` — the later system no longer makes the tested claim;
- `SUPERSEDED` — the obligation changed and an explicit relation connects old and new;
- `INVALIDATED` — the prior test or proof did not establish its stated claim;
- `UNRESOLVED` — the failure remains open.

### 9.2 Disposition record

When a claim with a failed receipt is removed or replaced, record:

```text
prior receipt id
prior claim id
new claim id, if any
disposition
reason
evidence
authorizing decision
time
```

A new claim receives no standing merely because it succeeds a withdrawn or invalidated claim.

### 9.3 Scope

Failure persistence applies to:

```text
target-model revisions
methodology revisions
specification revisions
runtime replacements
calibration and standing changes
```

## 10 · Comparison receipt

A comparison receipt contains:

```text
comparison_id
left and right receipt ids
compatibility result
lineage and dependence graph
coverage comparison
transformation or migration map
uncertainty ledgers
categorical status comparison
scalar relation, if authorized
grounding quality
standing and verdict authorization
explanation or refusal
```

A comparison never consists of a bare subtraction.
