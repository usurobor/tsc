# Foundation–Contract Reconciliation: Semantic Identity

**Version:** 0.1.0-draft
**Date:** July 2026
**Status:** Proposed design artifact for tsc#91
**Scope:** Defines the common semantic target from which the C≡, Core, Operational, CM, runtime, and example repairs must derive.

---

## 0. Named incoherence

TSC currently uses the same names—articulation, α, β, γ, coherence, and `C_Σ`—for materially different objects:

1. C≡ v3 represents a free ternary tree, then evaluates it by atom count, root occupancy, and a second count-derived quantity.
2. Core v3 defines perturbation stability, pairwise agreement, and temporal distributional similarity.
3. SELF-MEASURE asks one witness to assign three semantic scores to a static repository bundle.
4. The mechanical engine computes twelve Markdown and repository-structure proxies.
5. Philosophical and cellular-automata examples use persistence or visible order as an implicit coherence oracle.

The failure is not merely a wrong formula. Each layer constructs structured evidence and quotients it into a scalar before the common-source or lawful-continuation question is answered.

**Name:** premature quotienting.

A scalar may summarize a successful measurement receipt. It cannot replace the structure needed to establish the measurement.

## 1. Challenged assumptions

This design rejects the following assumptions of the current stack:

- a bare `tri(T,T,T)` is a sufficient representation of one-as-two;
- source, relation, and result are freely permutable semantic peers;
- triadicity fixes the number of views or observations at three;
- an associative commutative fold can measure tree shape or nesting depth;
- unarticulated `e` is therefore maximally coherent;
- agreement among summaries is sufficient evidence of one underlying process;
- retaining only alignment residuals is sufficient when the alignment maps carried the relation;
- a single universal scalar can preserve realizability, identifiability, lawful change, model insufficiency, and standing;
- final behavioral semantics may replace the concrete generator presentation without loss to complexity or causal questions.

## 2. Provisional semantic decisions

These decisions are the common target for the v4 alpha specifications. They remain provisional until the Game of Life conformance ascent and independent β review.

### D1 — Triadicity is role structure, not observation cardinality

The foundational triad is one typed articulation event:

```text
coherer --cohering--> cohered
source  -- relation --> result
```

There may be one, two, three, or continuously many observations. Their number is methodology-dependent.

### D2 — The middle is dependent, not a third substance

Use a dependent articulation type:

```text
Art = Σ(s : P). Σ(o : P). Rel(s,o)
```

Here `P` is the declared (possibly tagged/heterogeneous) pole universe. An event is `art(s, φ, o)` with `φ : Rel(s,o)`.

The relation is typed by both poles. It cannot be freely permuted with them. Earlier language that “the center carries unity” is migrated as follows:

> The center was right to be different in kind and wrong to localize unity in a slot. The middle carries the dependent cohering relation; unity belongs to the whole typed event and its lawful continuation.

### D3 — Concrete systems are open coalgebras

A deterministic concrete generator has the form:

```text
c : X × I → Art × X
```

or, when exponentials exist:

```text
c : X → (Art × X)^I
```

where:

- `X` is the concrete state or presentation space;
- `I` is the explicitly exogenous query/intervention channel relative to the declared model boundary;
- `Art` is the typed articulation event;
- the returned `X` is the continuation state.

Endogenous attention, action, and query generation belong in `X` or `Art`. Every CM declares `query_mode = exogenous | endogenous | mixed` and identifies the test-independent channel, if any.

### D4 — Final behavior is semantics, not the concrete whole

For the functor:

```text
F_I(Y) = (Art × Y)^I
```

if a final coalgebra exists:

```text
ζ      : νF_I → F_I(νF_I)
beh_c  : X → νF_I
```

with the commuting law:

```text
ζ ∘ beh_c = F_I(beh_c) ∘ c
```

Lambek’s lemma gives `ζ` as an isomorphism at the final behavior object. It does not make every concrete generator final or invertible.

The normative evidence retains the full commuting square:

```text
(X,c), beh_c, (νF_I,ζ), and the commutation witness
```

because final semantics intentionally quotients behaviorally equivalent concrete presentations, while complexity, implementation, and intervention claims are presentation-sensitive.

### D5 — Finality is conditional and scoped

Every CM declares:

- its carrier category or finite approximation;
- the chosen system functor;
- the existence theorem or constructive horizon used;
- any branching, cardinality, measurability, topology, or metric assumptions.

No CM may invoke a final behavior object merely because one is philosophically desirable.

### D6 — Articulation, source-proximity, and coherence are different constructs

```text
articulation
    what distinctions and transformations are present

source proximity
    how little articulation is present relative to a declared source state

coherence
    whether a differentiated generative presentation, its typed articulations,
    and its behavior remain one lawful process under the declared tests
```

C≡ v4 formalizes articulation. Core v4 formalizes coherence receipts. The current inverse-articulation witness may survive only under an explicitly different name; it is not a coherence axiom.

### D7 — A generator is the executable closure of a relational atlas

The relational atlas retains:

- correspondences and transformations;
- endpoints and role types;
- alternatives and uncertainty;
- path composition and cycle/global compatibility;
- unresolved correspondences;
- observation and intervention provenance.

A candidate generator is a compact, executable extension of this partial atlas to unobserved contexts. It is neither necessarily a concealed material object nor merely a lookup table of observed views.

### D8 — Identifiability is indexed by the intervention channel

For a declared CM, evidence, metric, tolerance, complexity bound, and input/intervention family `I`, the candidate fiber contains the observationally admissible generator presentations.

```text
empty fiber                 → NO_REALIZATION_IN_MODEL, only if emptiness is proved
multiple inequivalent       → UNDERDETERMINED
one equivalence class       → IDENTIFIED_IN_MODEL
search/evidence incomplete  → UNRESOLVED
```

Widening `I` may refine the fiber. A CM may not report absolute identification without declaring the query/intervention regime under which alternatives were excluded.

### D9 — Observation, active identification, and causal warrant are distinct

- Held-out passive observations can falsify behavioral prediction.
- Active interventions are required when competing generators are observationally equivalent under the passive regime.
- Causal attribution additionally depends on the declared model boundary and structural assumptions.

An intervention is exogenous relative to the tested boundary, not an absolute metaphysical outside. The larger observer–apparatus system may be modeled in a subsequent scope lift.

### D10 — Approximation has two compatible levels

Every empirical CM declares:

1. a path-tolerance monoid `(E, ⊕, 0, ≤)` for accumulated composition error;
2. a behavioral or bisimulation-compatible metric/pseudometric `d_B` for endpoint comparison;
3. a compatibility rule connecting local/path budgets to behavioral distance.

A fixed global epsilon with no path law is not an approximate-functoriality contract.

### D11 — The primary result is a receipt

The receipt retains:

```text
concrete presentation
behavioral semantics or finite-horizon approximation
commuting/compatibility witnesses
observations and input histories
relational atlas
candidate fiber and search-completeness claim
complexity accounting
held-out predictions/interventions
categorical statuses
standing and provenance
```

Any scalar summary is downstream, status-conditional, CM-local, and digest-linked to the receipt. It cannot turn `UNDERDETERMINED` into `IDENTIFIED`, `UNRESOLVED` into `NO_REALIZATION`, or `no standing` into authority.

### D12 — Law-relative coherence includes stochastic laws and lawful endings

Random-looking observations may be coherent with a declared stochastic generator. A negative control must violate its declared law, not merely lack persistent macroscopic form.

Identity may also end lawfully. `LAWFUL_TERMINATION` is distinct from `PROCESS_FAILURE` or declining coherence.

## 5. Conformance-first validation plan

Before promoting any v4 document to normative:

1. Generate a Game of Life conformance suite from code.
2. Prove static row/column projections are underdetermined in a known two-element fiber.
3. Add B3/S23 continuation and a held-out next projection; verify the fiber collapses to one candidate.
4. Generate a margin-preserving illegal transition; verify `LAW_VIOLATION` rather than a low visual-order score.
5. Generate an i.i.d. Bernoulli trace under an i.i.d. CM; verify compatibility with the law rather than universal failure.
6. Generate a lawful glider collision/termination case with a preregistered identity rule.
7. Run a second exact domain before claiming cross-domain generality.

The fixture may revise this design. It may not silently invent semantics absent from the design and then claim to have validated them.

## 6. Acceptance criteria for this design

- **AC1:** all downstream v4 specs use the same typed articulation kernel and do not reintroduce free semantic `S₃` permutation.
- **AC2:** C≡ contains no normative universal coherence scalar or `e = maximal coherence` axiom.
- **AC3:** Core retains both concrete presentations and behavioral semantics.
- **AC4:** identifiability is explicitly indexed by the declared input/intervention family.
- **AC5:** every approximate composition claim names both a path budget law and a compatible behavioral metric.
- **AC6:** the result schema distinguishes realizability, identifiability, continuation, model adequacy, lift, and standing.
- **AC7:** Game of Life conformance data and expected statuses are generated, not transcribed.
- **AC8:** current v3 implementation and ledger values are labeled as historical methodology outputs, not evidence for v4 validity.

## 7. Non-goals of the alpha draft

- proving reality is a final coalgebra;
- selecting the carrier category for every future CM;
- defining one universal behavioral metric;
- hard-coding cohomology or any one obstruction theory into Core;
- defining a universal scalar threshold;
- claiming the body application already satisfies the exact Set-based kernel;
- replacing v3 before the generated conformance suite and review complete.
