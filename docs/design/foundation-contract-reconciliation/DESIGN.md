# TSC v4 Foundation Revision

**Version:** 1.0.0-draft
**Status:** Design authority
**Target:** TSC 4.0.0

## Governing question

> What foundational change makes TSC's formal object, measurement theory, methodology contract, runtime, and examples describe one system?

TSC v4 replaces a scalar-first theory of coherence with a receipt-first theory of typed generative articulation. The revision begins from one primitive: a coherer and a cohered pole belong to one event only through a well-typed cohering relation. A concrete system repeatedly emits such events and continues. A coherence claim is warranted when a declared methodology preserves that generator, its relations, its candidate alternatives, and the evidence that tests its continuation.

---

## 1 · Pressure

The v3 stack used the same names for different constructs.

- C≡ treated coherence as proximity to unarticulated `e`.
- Core treated coherence as low discrepancy among summaries.
- The repository methodology treated coherence as a weighted reading of document features and semantic judgments.
- The cellular-automata examples treated visible persistent order as coherence.

Those quantities can disagree while each implementation remains internally faithful to its own definition. The stack therefore lacked one referent for `coherence`, `α`, `β`, `γ`, and `C_Σ`.

The recurring failure was **premature quotienting**:

```text
rich structure
  → count, mask, residual, or scalar
  → later claim that needs the discarded structure
```

The theory created relations, positions, alignments, histories, and alternatives, then removed them before deciding whether the observations belonged to one process.

## 2 · Demonstrated failures

### 2.1 Shape disappeared in the foundation

C≡ defined α and γ as associative commutative folds over child values.

Any evaluator of the form:

```text
E(e) = 1
E(a) = w_a
E(tri(x,y,z)) = E(x) ⊗ E(y) ⊗ E(z)
```

with associative commutative `⊗` factors through the multiset of leaves. It cannot detect arrangement, grouping, path, or depth.

For the v3 γ evaluator:

```text
E_γ(t) = (n(t), 2^n(t) - 1)
```

where `n(t)` is atom count. α was a capped function of the first γ coordinate. The purported three evaluators therefore exposed two information channels: atom multiplicity and a three-bit top-level occupancy mask.

Equal-count terms with different depth could receive identical α, β, and γ values.

### 2.2 Ternary meaning and binary evaluation conflicted

The foundation described `tri` as an irreducible holding of one-as-two, then evaluated it through repeated use of a binary monoid operation. The syntax claimed internal shape while the evaluators flattened it.

The center was also assigned two incompatible meanings:

- it carried unity;
- it was freely permutable with the other positions under `S₃`.

A component that differs in kind cannot be a free peer under semantic permutation.

### 2.3 Distinct constructs shared one score name

The v3 witness family made `e` maximally coherent and raised scores when articulation was removed. That is a source-proximity or non-articulation measure.

Core's score rose when summaries agreed or remained stable. That is a fit or stability measure.

The examples often rewarded persistent visible form. That is an order or motif-persistence measure.

Articulation extent, source proximity, inter-view fit, lawful continuation, and visible order are different constructs. Changing a sign cannot unify them.

### 2.4 The relation was discarded

Core computed an alignment `σ`, then retained a residual discrepancy while discarding the map that established the relation.

A circle and a triangle need not resemble one another to be lawful views of one generator. Their correspondence, projection geometry, alternatives, and composition conditions carry the evidence. A scalar residual cannot reconstruct or audit that relation.

### 2.5 Pairwise fit was mistaken for global fit

Pairwise-compatible maps may fail to form one global diagram. Conversely, multiple global realizations may fit all observations.

The stack did not preserve the difference among:

```text
no realization
several realizations
one identified realization
search unable to decide
```

A low score could mean contradiction, noise, model insufficiency, underdetermination, or an unimplemented observer. Those states require different actions.

### 2.6 Final behavior could repeat the same quotient error

Coalgebraic final semantics intentionally identifies behaviorally equivalent concrete states. That is useful for behavior, but it erases presentation facts needed for complexity, mechanism, intervention, and causal claims.

Keeping only `beh_c(x)` would make a presentation-sensitive complexity measure uncomputable. The normative object must therefore retain the commuting square:

```text
concrete presentation (X,c)
behavior map beh_c
behavior object νF or finite approximation
commuting witness
```

### 2.7 The runtime collapsed authority surfaces

The repository conflated or weakly separated:

```text
structural compilation
instrument assessment
admission policy
boundary authorization
target execution
```

A methodology can be well formed but inconsistent, consistent but uncalibrated, calibrated but out of scope, or useful without authority to issue a verdict. One status cannot represent those states.

### 2.8 Examples did not test the intended construct

The philosophical examples were illustrations with hand-authored data and expected scores.

The cellular-automata files contained hand-transcribed oracle material rather than generated evidence. Random independent frames were treated as a universal negative even though an i.i.d. Bernoulli trace can be lawful under an i.i.d. methodology.

The mechanical katas tested document-scoring regressions, not coherence of cellular dynamics.

The examples therefore could not falsify the general measurement claim.

## 3 · Challenged assumptions

The revision rejects these assumptions:

1. **Three free positions are the primitive.**
   The primitive is a typed relation whose type depends on its poles.

2. **The center stores unity.**
   The middle role stores the dependent cohering relation. Unity belongs to the complete event and its lawful continuation.

3. **A commutative fold can measure internal shape.**
   Shape requires a genuine tree or diagram algebra that retains node and path structure.

4. **Coherence is one context-free scalar.**
   Coherence is a scoped disposition supported by a proof-carrying receipt.

5. **Agreement between views establishes one object.**
   A common-source claim requires a declared generator class, maps, global compatibility, complexity control, and an oracle.

6. **A generator is a hidden material object.**
   A generator is a lawful presentation that produces and transforms observations. Material objects are one possible domain realization.

7. **Final behavior is the whole normative object.**
   Final behavior is one quotient. The concrete presentation and semantics map remain normative where later claims depend on them.

8. **Observation count determines reconstructive power.**
   Identifiability depends jointly on the generator class, observation operators, correspondence structure, noise, and intervention family.

9. **Random-looking means incoherent.**
   Coherence is relative to a declared law. A stochastic generator may be lawful.

10. **Compilation grants epistemic authority.**
    Compilation, assessment, admission, authorization, and execution are separate surfaces.

## 4 · Candidate boundary moves

### 4.1 Patch the scalar theory

This move would replace γ, repair formulas, retain alignment maps, and add more verdict states while keeping the term algebra and scalar aggregate central.

It was rejected because the primitive would still make three free peers foundational, the score would still carry several constructs, and every later feature would work around the same early loss of structure.

### 4.2 Add structured receipts above the existing foundation

This move would preserve v3 terms but place atlases, candidate fibers, and standing around them.

It was rejected because the receipt would contradict its own foundation: the upper layer would require typed paths and generator presentations that the lower layer had already erased.

### 4.3 Rebuild from typed articulation and open generation

This move replaces the primitive and moves scalar summaries to the edge.

It was selected because it changes the boundary that generated the repeated failures:

```text
articulation foundation
  owns typed events, paths, generators, and behavioral semantics

Core
  owns realizability, identifiability, continuation, and receipt obligations

CM
  owns domain observation, generator class, metrics, complexity, and oracle

Operational
  owns compilation, assessment, admission, authorization, and execution

Observation Dynamics
  owns lineage, comparison, dependence, intervention, and lift
```

## 5 · Selected foundation

### 5.1 Typed articulation

Let `P` be a pole type and:

```text
Rel : P × P → Set
```

be a dependent relation family.

Define:

```text
Art := Σ(s : P). Σ(o : P). Rel(s,o)
```

An event:

```text
art(s, φ, o)
```

contains:

```text
s                coherer/source role
φ : Rel(s,o)     cohering/dependent relation
o                cohered/result role
```

The relation cannot be permuted into a pole position because its type depends on both poles.

### 5.2 Open generator

A concrete generator is:

```text
c : X × I → Art × X
```

with initial state `x_0`.

The input `I` is exogenous relative to the tested boundary. A methodology declares whether its vantage or query is exogenous, endogenous, or mixed.

### 5.3 Behavioral semantics

For:

```text
F_I(Y) := (Art × Y)^I
```

`c` is a coalgebra:

```text
c : X → F_I(X)
```

When a final coalgebra exists:

```text
ζ : νF_I → F_I(νF_I)
beh_c : X → νF_I
ζ ∘ beh_c = F_I(beh_c) ∘ c
```

The structure map `ζ` is an isomorphism. Complete behavior and one articulation plus continuation are mutually recoverable at final behavior.

A concrete generator is not presumed final. A CM that uses final behavior must declare its category, functor, and existence result. Otherwise it declares a finite or approximate behavior construction.

### 5.4 Candidate fiber

A methodology first constructs candidates from fit and calibration evidence:

```text
F_M^train(D) = {
  [G]_≃M |
  fit_M(G,D_train) ≤ τ_M and K_M(G) ≤ κ_M
}
```

Held-out and intervention evidence then refines that fixed candidate set without refitting:

```text
F_M^test(D) = {
  [G]_≃M ∈ F_M^train(D) |
  oracle_M(G,D_test) = PASS
}
```

Each applicable fiber supports distinct results:

```text
empty, proved          NO_REALIZATION_IN_MODEL
multiple classes       UNDERDETERMINED
one class              IDENTIFIED_IN_MODEL
not established        UNRESOLVED
```

Identification is indexed by the declared passive and active input family. Training compatibility and held-out support remain separate claims.

### 5.5 Structured verification receipts

The verification triad remains as three non-interchangeable receipt roles:

```text
α  manifestation validity and coverage
β  relational atlas and candidate fiber
γ  lawful continuation and held-out prediction
```

These are not three observed views. A CM may have any number of observation channels.

### 5.6 Receipt primacy

The primary result retains:

```text
concrete generator presentation
behavior reference or approximation
behavior map / commuting evidence
observations and provenance
relational atlas
candidate fiber and alternatives
continuation and intervention tests
complexity and uncertainty
standing and authority
```

A scalar may summarize a receipt only after the categorical status is fixed. It cannot replace the receipt or alter its status.

## 6 · Invariants

The revision must preserve these invariants.

### I1 · Relation-first typing

No articulation event is well formed without a relation typed by both poles.

### I2 · Structural retention

No count, mask, residual, final behavior, or scalar replaces structured evidence unless it is proved sufficient for the exact downstream claim.

### I3 · Scoped claims

Every coherence claim names its CM, generator class, input family, equivalence, tolerances, complexity rule, oracle, and standing.

### I4 · Realizability before scoring

The system classifies the candidate fiber before producing any optional scalar summary.

### I5 · Global compatibility

Pairwise fit never substitutes for full-diagram compatibility.

### I6 · Identifiability is input-indexed

`IDENTIFIED_IN_MODEL` is invalid without a declared input/intervention family.

### I7 · Law-relative continuation

Randomness, change, collision, or termination are not failures by appearance. The declared generator law determines whether continuation is lawful.

### I8 · Authority separation

Compilation does not imply assessment, admission, authorization, or standing.

### I9 · Honest approximation

Approximate composition declares both a path-tolerance calculus and a compatible endpoint behavioral metric.

### I10 · Finality is conditional

A final behavior claim names the category, functor, and existence basis. Otherwise the receipt states its finite or approximate scope.

## 8 · Conformance strategy

The first conformance suite uses generated Game of Life data because its generator and oracle are exact.

### 8.1 Static underdetermination

Row and column margins admit two distinct grids.

Expected result:

```text
COMPATIBLE_IN_MODEL
UNDERDETERMINED
```

### 8.2 Dynamic identification

The two static candidates predict different next margins under B3/S23. A held-out next observation selects one candidate.

Expected result:

```text
SUPPORTED_IN_MODEL
IDENTIFIED_IN_MODEL
LIFT_VALIDATED
```

### 8.3 Law violation with preserved surface statistics

A switched successor preserves live-cell count and selected margins while violating B3/S23.

Expected result:

```text
REFUTED_IN_MODEL
LAW_VIOLATION
```

The oracle proves that TSC checks the declared law rather than visual order.

### 8.4 Lawful stochastic process

An i.i.d. Bernoulli trace is measured under an i.i.d. Bernoulli CM.

Expected result:

```text
SUPPORTED_IN_MODEL
```

Identification may remain underdetermined for finite data. Random appearance is not a negative oracle.

### 8.5 Lawful termination

A generated collision is tested under a preregistered identity and termination rule.

Expected result:

```text
LAWFUL_TERMINATION
```

Disappearance of the identified motif is not automatically coherence degradation.

### 8.6 Negative foundation tests

The suite must also reject:

```text
free permutation of source/relation/result roles
commutative-fold claims of shape sensitivity
scalar-only receipts
identification without an input family
comparison across incompatible CMs
lift claims without preregistration and held-out prediction
```

A second domain must pass before TSC claims domain-generality.

## 9 · Leverage

### Positive leverage

The selected move:

- gives one formal referent to coherer, cohering, and cohered;
- makes invalid role permutations unrepresentable;
- preserves the maps needed for reconstruction and audit;
- distinguishes contradiction from underdetermination and model insufficiency;
- supports deterministic, stochastic, passive, active, and reflexive systems under one kernel;
- gives CM compilation a truthful target contract;
- lets examples falsify the theory instead of merely illustrating it;
- makes future domain work an extension of CM declarations rather than another Core rewrite.

### Negative leverage

The move:

- removes simple universal score comparison;
- requires more structured receipts and larger artifacts;
- makes finality and approximation obligations explicit;
- requires each CM to declare a generator class, equivalence, complexity rule, and oracle;
- requires the body application to earn an appropriate metric or measurable category rather than inheriting `Set` semantics;
- increases the work needed before a measurement may carry standing.

These costs are the price of retaining the information that the claim depends on.

## 10 · Replacement policy

TSC 4.0.0 is a clean replacement of the foundational specification set.

The normative specifications define only the current model. They contain no compatibility layer and no narrative of earlier versions. Git retains prior specifications and their rationale.

This design document owns the revision rationale. It does not govern the mathematical definitions once the normative specifications are accepted.

Existing measurements remain historical records of the instruments that produced them. They do not inherit v4 semantics.

## 11 · Acceptance criteria

### AC1 · Standalone specifications

Each normative spec begins from its governing question, defines its primitives before use, and contains no revision history or compatibility prose.

**Check:** search the spec tree for historical-version narration; result is empty.

### AC2 · Typed articulation

`spec/c-equiv.md` defines `Rel`, `Art`, open generators, coalgebraic behavior, equivalence, and no-premature-quotient obligations.

**Positive:** `art(s,φ,o)` typechecks only when `φ : Rel(s,o)`.
**Negative:** free permutation of `φ` into a pole role is rejected.

### AC3 · Candidate-fiber semantics

`spec/tsc-core.md` defines separate realizability, identifiability, continuation, and unresolved statuses.

**Positive:** two lawful candidates produce `UNDERDETERMINED`.
**Negative:** a scalar threshold cannot relabel the fiber status.

### AC4 · Receipt primacy

Every normative result schema retains its presentation, atlas, alternatives, continuation evidence, uncertainty, and standing.

**Negative:** a scalar-only result fails conformance.

### AC5 · Authority separation

`spec/tsc-oper.md` distinguishes compilation, sandbox calibration, CM0 assessment, validation, boundary authorization, and execution.

**Negative:** compilation alone cannot produce target verdict authority.

### AC6 · Observation accountability

`spec/tsc-observation-dynamics.md` records boundary, query ownership, dependence, tolerance, behavioral metric, comparison compatibility, and lift provenance.

**Negative:** a held-out claim produced by the candidate under test fails the independence check unless explicitly modeled.

### AC7 · Generated conformance suite

Game of Life conformance data, candidate fibers, transitions, margins, and expected statuses are generated from code and verified in CI.

No human-transcribed frame or hand-selected `C_Σ` is an oracle.

### AC8 · Artifact-role separation

Illustrations, regressions, conformance fixtures, calibration anchors, and experiments have distinct locations and schemas.

### AC9 · Cross-surface alignment

README, architecture, schemas, runtime, reports, and examples describe the same v4 contract before release.

### AC10 · Independent review

A β review challenges the foundation with at least:

```text
a type-level counterexample attempt
a candidate-fiber counterexample
a stochastic-law case
an intervention-equivalence case
a scalar-quotient attack
```

Release requires every D-level finding to have a positive and negative regression.
