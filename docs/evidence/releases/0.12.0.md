## Outcome

Coherence delta: C_Σ A− (`α A−`, `β A`, `γ A−`) · **Level:** L7

The meter's authority became **truthfully bounded**, and the tenant's CDD
process became **current and mechanically enforced**. The pre-registered
factorized-β experiment measured — and honestly recorded as a terminal
**FAIL** — that same-route LLM semantic judgment is not intrinsically
stable enough to be a scalar consistency authority: even with the engine
choosing identical loci for every witness and the LLM restricted to a
bounded verdict, independent witnesses still disagree on the local
semantic relation itself (gate A3). Separately, the CDD skill bundle is
refreshed onto cnos 3.82.0, vendored with an integrity manifest and CI
verifier. Nothing over-claims: the meter is now retained as measurement
infrastructure with no consistency claim.

## Why it matters

"Can we make the semantic witness self-consistent enough to bear a scalar
score?" was the open question behind three prior attempts. This release
closes it with evidence — a genuinely different attack (remove the LLM's
scalar judgment; fix the loci; aggregate mechanically) that still failed —
and pivots the useful work to the meter as a **defect-proposer and
ambiguity-detector**, treating disagreement as a measured property of the
artifact (successor line #76). The platform refresh makes the process the
repo actually runs under current, integrity-checked, and gate-enforced.
